Privacy Policy – Little Pilot

Effective Date: 07-March-2026

Little Pilot is a mobile game developed by Ofizu Games. This privacy policy explains what information we may collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1️⃣ Information We Collect

Little Pilot does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) from children or adults.

During gameplay, we collect the following data on the local device only:

  • In-game scores and collection progress
  • Gameplay behavior (e.g., which animals were collected, how long the game was played)

This information is only used to show game statistics to the player. It is not shared with any third party.

2️⃣ Third-Party Services

  • The game does not include any third-party services that identify users personally.
  • No personal information from children is collected.
  • The game does not include advertisements.

3️⃣ Data Security

  • Game data is stored securely on the device and is not shared with third parties.

4️⃣ Children’s Privacy

Little Pilot is designed for children under 13 and complies with COPPA (USA) and applicable privacy laws.

  • No personal data is requested
  • Any social or interactive features are child-appropriate
  • No advertisements are shown in the game
  • Suitable for all children (PEGI 3 rating)

Note: While designed for children under 13, anyone can play the game.

5️⃣ User Rights

  • Players can view their game data
  • For any information, contact us at: ofizu@ofizu.com

6️⃣ Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated over time. Updates will be posted in the app or on the Store listing.
