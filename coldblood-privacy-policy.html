COLDBLOOD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Summary

COLDBLOOD is a single-player game. The current version of the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection to play.

What the app does not do (today)

  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No data is sent to any server
  • No advertising

Planned features and future data collection

This policy is written in advance to cover features planned for future versions of COLDBLOOD. If and when these features are added, they will work as described below. This policy will be updated with a new "last updated" date whenever that happens, and updates will not reduce your rights under this policy.

Advertising

Not yet implemented. When added, COLDBLOOD may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other technical data (such as device type and general location) to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy. On Android, you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's system settings at any time.

Leaderboards and score tracking

Not yet implemented. When added, leaderboard features are expected to use Google Play Games Services. In that case, your Google Play Games profile (such as your public in-game name) and game scores may be stored and displayed to other players through Google's service. This data is managed by Google under its own privacy policy, linked above. You will be able to see which Google account is connected through the Play Games Services interface itself.

Data we do not collect, now or in the future

Regardless of future updates, COLDBLOOD will not request access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera, and will never sell your data to third parties.

Children's privacy

COLDBLOOD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ofizu@ofizu.com.

COLDBLOOD is developed by Ofizu Games.
